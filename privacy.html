Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The Pinchshot app does not collect, send or share any of your personal data. Everything the game uses stays on your headset, and uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

About

This policy explains how Pinchshot (“the app”), made by Sergei Galkin (“we”, “us”), handles your information. It applies to the Pinchshot app for Meta Quest and to this website.

Data the app uses

To work, the app uses the following data. All of it is processed only on your headset:

  • Hand tracking. The positions of your hands and fingers, provided in real time by your headset, so you can play with your hands. This data is used live and is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Room scan (Space Setup). The layout of your room, such as walls, floor and furniture, provided by your headset, so levels can be built around your space. The app saves level layouts for each room on your headset, linked to an ID of the room scan.
  • Game progress and settings. Which levels you have passed and your in-game settings, saved on your headset.

Data we collect

None. The app does not send any data to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect names, email addresses, device IDs, voice, images or location.

Why it is used

Only to run the game: to let you play with your hands, to build levels that fit your room, and to remember your progress and settings. We do not use your data for anything else.

Sharing

We do not share or sell any data. The app sends us nothing, and we only receive what you choose to email us. Your headset and the Meta Horizon Store are run by Meta, which handles data under its own privacy policy.

If you email us

When you write to pinchshotxr@gmail.com, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you and to fix problems you report, and we never share them. Ask us at any time and we will delete them.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not store anything in your browser. All of its files, including fonts and video, are served from this site. It is hosted on GitHub Pages: like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to run and protect the service, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not have access to those logs.

Deleting your data

All data the app stores is on your headset. To delete it, uninstall Pinchshot from your headset, or clear the app's data in your headset settings. This removes your progress, settings and saved room layouts. Your room scan itself belongs to your headset's Space Setup and can be deleted there.

We do not hold any of your data on our side. If you still have a question or a deletion request, email us at pinchshotxr@gmail.com. This is free and available to everyone, wherever you live.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live.
